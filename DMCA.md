# DMCA (Digital Millennium Copyright Act) Policy

FolkUp Ecosystem respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects
users of our repositories and hosted content to do the same. In accordance with
the Digital Millennium Copyright Act of 1998 (17 U.S.C. § 512), FolkUp will
respond promptly to claims of copyright infringement committed using our
repositories that are reported to our designated agent identified below.

## Reporting Copyright Infringement

### Via GitHub (recommended primary channel)

This repository is hosted on GitHub, which processes DMCA takedown notices per
their [DMCA Takedown Policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/dmca-takedown-policy).

To submit a DMCA takedown notice:

1. Visit https://github.com/contact/dmca
2. Follow the GitHub DMCA takedown notice form
3. GitHub will notify us and process the notice per their procedure

### Direct to FolkUp Ecosystem

If you prefer to contact us directly before filing a formal takedown notice
(for quick resolution or clarification questions), reach us at:

**Email:** `info@folkup.app`

Please include in your inquiry:

- Description of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed
- Location (URL) of the allegedly infringing material in this repository
- Your contact information (name, email, physical address, phone)
- A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not
  authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
- A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is
  accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the
  copyright owner's behalf
- Your physical or electronic signature

We aim to respond within 5 business days.

## Counter-Notification

If you believe your content was removed in error, you may file a counter-notice.
Follow GitHub's counter-notice procedure at:
https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/dmca-counter-notice-policy

## Repeat Infringer Policy

FolkUp Ecosystem reserves the right to terminate access for users who are
determined to be repeat infringers of copyright.
